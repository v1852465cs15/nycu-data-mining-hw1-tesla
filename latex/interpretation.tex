At the training mean registration year, Model X and S contrasts are EUR 24,759 and 20,308 relative to Model 3 (Table~\ref{tab:coef}). The common year slope is EUR 1,882 per year newer; S and X add 2,296 and 3,112 EUR/year. This supports heterogeneous slopes, not a causal effect of aging. The Model Y interaction interval crosses zero, so the group gain does not establish a distinct Y slope.

Mileage contributes about EUR -590 per additional 10,000 km. A Performance mention corresponds to about EUR 2,941 holding the other terms fixed. AWD has a negative conditional coefficient despite its intuitive premium: it overlaps power, model and trim flags, and is a seller-text indicator. VIF reaches 5.9, so correlated design and omitted trim details are plausible explanations. Equipment and EAP intervals cross zero; retaining them preserves the matched raw-feature baseline, not a claim that they are individually essential.
