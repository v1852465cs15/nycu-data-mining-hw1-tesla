\paragraph{Interpretation.} Each newer year adds EUR 1,882 for Model 3, about EUR 4,178 for S and EUR 4,994 for X; Y does not differ from Model 3. This supports heterogeneous slopes, not a causal effect of aging. Trim matters beyond power and model: HW4, new generation, Performance and Long Range carry premiums and the entry trim a discount (Table~\ref{tab:coef}). Italy (+EUR 2,775), Spain ($-$EUR 1,430) and Belgium ($-$EUR 842) differ from the Netherlands; other countries do not. FSD adds EUR 866; EAP is not significant.

AWD ($-$EUR 1,461) corrects for overlap: 88\% of AWD-flagged listings also carry Long Range or Performance and report more power (318 vs 282 kW), so those terms take most of the premium; a Performance AWD car still nets +EUR 1,480. Raw AWD listings are also cheaper (EUR 34,596 vs EUR 36,016), so the sign is not a reversal.
